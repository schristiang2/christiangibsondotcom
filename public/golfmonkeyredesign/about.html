About

The man behind the monkey.

Bob Merryman. Twenty-five years as a Charleston-area club pro. Two albatrosses. Zero interest in pretending this game is easy.

The front nine

  1. ServiceU.S. Navy — Gulf War, Somalia & nude beaches in Spain.
  2. 2000sHead Golf Professional & GM, Berkeley Country Club — turned a club in the country into a country club with an edge.
  3. 2014Director of Golf, Legend Oaks — and lost a charity match, one-armed, to a partially paralyzed Navy vet, on purpose-ish, for Folds of Honor.
  4. 2019An albatross — bringing the crazy-hole count to 2 albatrosses, 4 holes-in-one & 1 quintuple bogey.
  5. 2020Founded The Golfhouse — golf studio & private simulator club. Masks optional, fun mandatory.
  6. 2022Daddy Caddie — looped for daughter Aubs (Coastal Carolina University) to her first of three Charleston City Championships.
  7. 2026Sold The Golfhouse to do the two things he loves most: teaching this game, and refusing to take it seriously. Golf Monkey is the result. Still tees it up in U.S. Open qualifiers, hoping to catch lightning in a bottle.
A story from the parking lot

The oak tree.

Bob was general manager of a country club, walking to his car, when a member caught him in the parking lot to complain about pace of play. Bob stopped listening and looked off to the side.

“How old do you think that oak tree is?”

Two hundred fifty years, maybe, the member said, and went back to pace of play. Bob looked over at the ninth green. The sun was setting.

“Should we watch the sunset over this green together?”

The member looked at him. “I get it.”

“Hey, man, it’s really nice out here. You should enjoy your day a little bit more.”

Making the turn

The front nine? He’s proud of it. A few birdies, a few mistakes, a couple he’d love to have back. But they call it the turn for a reason. Golf Monkey is a career club pro grabbing a snack, warning the clubhouse the course record has about two hours to live, and walking to the 10th tee to play the best nine holes of his life.

It starts with instruction that moves the needle, merch worth wearing and a podcast that wouldn’t be made if it didn’t deserve to exist. Maybe a tour — if the napkin survives — and hopefully a university for aspiring kids and parents seeking answers. Underneath all of it, the same thing Bob has done his whole life: helping people fall back in love with an impossible game.

What is the Monkey?

Golf is the most beautiful absurdity mankind has ever agreed to take seriously. We lay our fit out the night before a tee time. We whisper on the tee box and fist pump on greens. And then we chunk a 60-yard wedge into a pond.

Strip away the gear and the rituals, and what’s left? A primate swinging a stick. That’s why our Monkey wears a tuxedo — golf is the whole animal dressed for dinner. Polished on the outside, bananas on the inside.

Scratch or thirty, private or muni, ten million in the bank or twenty-five dollars for one great monkey t-shirt — if you can laugh at yourself between the ropes, you’re already one of us.

You’re a Golf Monkey. Welcome to the Troop.

— Pro
