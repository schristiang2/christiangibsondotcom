Meet your trainer

Brandon Nurnberger

Brandon speaks golf and fitness. He doesn’t hand you a generic gym plan. He figures out what your body can and can’t do, then trains it to swing the club the way you want.

He’s been in strength and conditioning his whole career. After a kinesiology degree from SUNY Cortland, he opened Relentless Strength & Performance, a semi-private gym on Long Island, and spent nearly a decade helping hundreds of people get stronger.

Then golf got him. Years of college football and strongman hadn’t built a body for the golf swing, so he learned everything he could about golf-specific mobility, and noticed how badly golfers are underserved when it comes to fitness.

How he got here

From the weight room to the range

2011

Opens Relentless

Kinesiology degree in hand, he opens a strength and conditioning gym on Long Island.

2016

Picks up golf

Starts playing for fun. By 2019 he’s at the range, taking lessons and hooked.

2021

Moves to Summerville

Sells his stake in Relentless and joins P4S Golf, the world’s largest golf strength and conditioning company, as a remote golf performance coach.

2022

Meets Bob

Joins The Golfhouse in Ladson and meets Bob. Starts coaching golfers on his own, in Summerville and remotely.

Now

Golf Monkey Fitness

Two guys with the same passion for helping people, one for the swing and one for the body behind it.

At P4S he coached dozens of golfers, from former pros to weekend warriors.

What you’ll train

Swing speed

Speed and power work so the club moves faster without you swinging harder.

Flexibility

Hips, thoracic spine and shoulders that turn when you ask them to.

Agility & balance

Stable feet, a steady finish and a body that holds its posture through impact.

Less pain

Strength that keeps your back happy and you playing for as long as you want.

How it works

01

Get screened

03

Train & measure

Train with Brandon, re-test, and watch the numbers move — mobility, balance and clubhead speed.
