Website Brief

A redesign of burdenedhope.com built around Series 01: The Foundation.

See the live preview

Designed and built by Christian Gibson · November 23, 2026

01

Home

The story first, the products second.

  • Clear, then blackThe nav is see-through over the hero image, then turns solid black once you scroll past it.
  • Fits every phoneOn phones, the hero is sized to each visitor’s exact screen height, so the first thing they see is always the full hero.
  • Heavy and lightEvery headline pairs a heavy blackletter word with a light serif one, the same pairing as the logo.
  • The markThe cross-through-O from the chest print sits top left, opposite the cart.

02

Shop

Collection 01: Burden, with a page for every product.

  • FiltersTees, Outerwear and Home.
  • Every product carries its verseMatthew 11:28 or Luke 22:42.
  • Product pagesPicking a color swaps the photo and the price updates by size.
  • The meaning up frontWhat the design means sits right next to the buy button.

03 · The standout

The Gospel

Seven chapters, told the way the brand is built: the page gets darker through Sin and Judgment, then light breaks in at Christ and stays.

ChapterWhat happens as you scroll
02 SinA line draws across and stops short of “the glory of God” (Romans 3:23)
03 JudgmentThe word tightens and settles into place
04 ChristLight opens out from the center and pushes the black away
05 The CrossA cross draws itself in two strokes, and “Death” is struck through
06 ResponseThe “Turn” arrow spins around
  • Fast by designEach effect finishes within a short stretch of scrolling, before you start reading. They switch off for anyone whose phone is set to reduce motion.
  • Scripture on tapEvery chapter has a “Read the Scripture” button with four verses, all in the LSB.
  • A next stepIt ends with Begin with John, linked to the Gospel of John.

04

Built for the phone

Every page is designed for phone and desktop. The nav sits under the logo, grids stack, and the Gospel chapters turn into a simple list.
