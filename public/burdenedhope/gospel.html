The Gospel

The good news of Jesus Christ, and the hope found in Him alone.

01God 02Sin 03Judgment 04Christ 05The Cross 06Response 07Hope
01 — The Beginning

God

The Gospel begins with God. He created all things and all people for His glory.

He is holy, righteous, and good, and everything belongs to Him. We were created to know Him, worship Him, and live in fellowship with Him.

02 — The Problem

Sin

Sin is not merely the wrong things we do. It is rebellion against the holy God who made us.

God created mankind to know Him and live according to His will, but humanity rebelled against Him. Every person has sinned and falls short of His glory, and sin separates us from God.

03 — The Consequence

Judgment

Because God is holy and just, sin cannot simply be ignored.

Every person will stand before God and give an account. His judgment is righteous, and the consequence of sin is death and separation from Him.

But God did not leave us without hope.

04 — The Answer

Christ

Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, took on flesh and lived the life we could not live: perfectly obedient and without sin. He came to save sinners and to reconcile us to God.

05 — The Victory

The Cross & Resurrection

At the cross, Jesus willingly gave His life for sinners, bearing the judgment our sin deserves. He died and was buried. On the third day, He rose bodily from the grave, conquering death and securing salvation for all who belong to Him.

Death could not hold Him.

06 — The Response

Repentance & Faith

Turn

Repentance is turning from sin toward God.

Trust

Faith is trusting in Christ alone for salvation, not in our own works, goodness, or ability to make ourselves right with God.

07 — The Hope

Come to Christ

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” Matthew 11:28

Jesus does not call sinners to make themselves worthy before coming to Him. He calls the weary and burdened to come to Him.

Those who come to Christ in repentance and faith are forgiven, reconciled to God, and given eternal life. Our hope is found in Christ alone.
